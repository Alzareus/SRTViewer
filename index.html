Privacy Policy — SRT Viewer

Last updated: 12 June 2026

SRT Viewer ("the app") is a tool that plays and monitors SRT (Secure Reliable Transport) live video streams that you choose to connect to. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party tracking in the app.

Data stored on your device

  • Connection settings (host, port, mode, stream ID, passphrase, latency and buffer values) are stored locally on your device only, so the configuration screen can be pre-filled. They never leave your device and are not sent to the developer.
  • Statistics logs (CSV and HTML reports) are generated only when you tap the "Record stats" button. They are written to the app's private storage on your device and are shared with other apps only when you explicitly use the share button.

Network connections

The app connects only to the SRT server address that you enter. The audio and video of that stream are decoded and displayed on your device in real time; they are not recorded, uploaded, or stored by the app. Your SRT passphrase, if used, is sent only to the server you specify, as part of the standard SRT handshake.

Permissions

The app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE solely to connect to the SRT stream you configure.

Third-party open-source components

The app includes the open-source libraries libVLC (VideoLAN) and libsrt / srtdroid for decoding and transport. These run entirely on your device and do not collect personal data.

Children

The app is a professional/technical utility and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because the developer collects no personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our side. You can clear all locally stored settings and logs at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
